## What I’m Working On

**[ANVO LegalAI](https://github.com/)** — Bangladesh’s first AI-powered legal intelligence platform.

A production RAG system over **35,000+ Bangladeshi legal documents** spanning 235 years of case law and legislation. Built a dual-LLM fallback architecture (Groq Llama 3.3 + Gemini 2.5 Flash), BGE-M3 embeddings, FAISS vector store, and ParentDocumentRetriever — all behind a FastAPI backend with end-to-end encrypted storage on Supabase.

Live. Paying clients. Running in production.

The idea came from growing up in a family of lawyers and watching ordinary people get completely lost in the legal system. I wanted to fix that.

## Research

**Adaptive Weighted Ensemble of CNNs with Focal Loss and Grad-CAM** *(Under Review, Springer — Pattern Recognition and Image Analysis)*

First-authored a skin lesion classification study on HAM10000. Ensemble of ResNet34, ResNet50, and EfficientNet-B0 with adaptive soft-voting. Used Focal Loss to handle brutal class imbalance. Integrated Grad-CAM so doctors can actually *see* why the model made a call — not just trust a number.

→ **79.74% test accuracy**, **0.7782 weighted F1** — ~3% above every individual base model.

I care about explainable AI because a model a doctor doesn’t trust is a model that doesn’t get used.
